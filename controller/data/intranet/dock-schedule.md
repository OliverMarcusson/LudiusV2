Inbound deliveries are booked in 30-minute dock slots, docks 1-3 for board reels and pallets, dock 4 for outbound. A haulier more than 30 minutes late loses the slot and is fitted in when a dock is free.

Repeated late arrivals on a dock are raised with the plant manager with the dates and the haulier, so the slot times can be changed.
