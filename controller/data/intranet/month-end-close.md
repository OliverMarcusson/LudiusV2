Working day 1: purchase ledger cut-off, goods received not invoiced accrual, payroll journal. Working day 2: bank and intercompany reconciliations, stock valuation from the warehouse count. Working day 3: accruals and prepayments review, fixed asset register, VAT control account.

Working day 4: workpaper pack complete and reviewed, management accounts draft with commentary on variances over EUR 10,000 or 10%. Working day 5: final pack to the finance director.

Workpapers live in the Finance share under Close\<year>\<month>. Every reconciliation shows the preparer, the reviewer and the date.
