Under Dutch GAAP (RJ 210 Intangible fixed assets) we capitalise internally developed software only in the development phase, when technical feasibility, the intention and ability to complete and use it, probable future economic benefits and a reliable measure of the cost are all shown. Research-phase costs are expensed.

Cloud ERP: the subscription is a service, not an asset; fees are expensed over the contract. Configuration and customisation that we do not control as a separate asset (most SaaS set-up work) is expensed when the service is received. Data migration, training and parallel running are always expensed.

Costs that can qualify: external developer time on interfaces we own, internal staff time booked to the build project codes, and testing before go-live. Keep timesheets per project code; without them nothing is capitalised.

The policy note on the ERP finance module project is drafted by the financial controller and reviewed with the finance director before the management accounts. Questions: group finance.
