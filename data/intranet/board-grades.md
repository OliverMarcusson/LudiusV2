Liners we buy: kraftliner (virgin fibre, strongest, for wet and heavy loads) and testliner (recycled). Common grammages: 125, 150 and 175 gsm. Fluting: 100-140 gsm semi-chemical or recycled.

Equivalents when switching supplier: compare grammage, burst strength and SCT/ECT values, not only the grade name. A 150 gsm kraftliner from a new mill is accepted after a trial run on line 2 and quality's sign-off.

Standard reel widths are 1,800, 2,100 and 2,500 mm. Confirm widths with planning before the first order.
