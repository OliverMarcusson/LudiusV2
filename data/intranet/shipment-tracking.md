Our carriers report pickups and proof of delivery into the order system each evening. For a shipment that left today, ask the warehouse shift lead for the loading list; for older ones, check the order's delivery tab.

If a customer reports a missing delivery, open a carrier claim within 7 days with the order number, pallet count and pickup date.
