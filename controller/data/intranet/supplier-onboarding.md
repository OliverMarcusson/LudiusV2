Before a supplier is created in the payables ledger, accounts payable checks the KvK extract (name, registered address, KvK number), the VAT number against the EU VIES register, and the IBAN against a letter or invoice on the supplier's letterhead.

Bank detail changes are always confirmed by phone with a known contact, on a number taken from our own records, never from the email asking for the change.

The procurement owner fills in the supplier request form; AP creates the account. Purchase orders are required for all new board suppliers from the first delivery.
