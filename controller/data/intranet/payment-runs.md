Payment runs are on Tuesday and Friday. Invoices need a matching purchase order and goods receipt (three-way match); anything else goes to the budget holder for approval in the workflow.

Disputed invoices are put on hold with a reason code and the buyer is told the same day. Open items over 60 days are reviewed at the month-end close.
