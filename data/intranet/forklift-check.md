Every shift, before first use: forks and carriage for cracks and wear, chains and mast, tyres, hydraulic leaks, battery charge and connector, horn, lights and beacon, brakes and parking brake, seat belt, steering, and the data plate is readable.

Record the check on the truck's card. A defect that affects safety takes the truck out of use: tag it and report it to the shift lead; do not drive it.

Annual fleet safety refresh: all drivers repeat the practical assessment this autumn. The schedule is on the warehouse notice board.
