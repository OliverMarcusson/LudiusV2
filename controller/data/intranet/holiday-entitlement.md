Dutch law gives every employee statutory holiday of four times the weekly working hours a year: 20 days for a full-time 5-day week. Our collective agreement adds 5 non-statutory days, so a full-timer has 25 days.

Statutory days not taken expire six months after the end of the year they were earned in; non-statutory days expire after five years. Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead; your manager approves.

During the office consolidation, leave in the move week is only refused for the move team. Ask HR about special leave (moving house, family events).
