Head office moves from the second and third floors to the third floor only. Teams pack their desks into the labelled crates the Friday before their move date; IT moves the PCs and phones over the weekend.

Desks are allocated by team zone, not personally. Badge access for the new floor is set up by facilities with IT; request changes through the service desk with the colleague's name and team.

Meeting rooms on the second floor close after the move. Book the new rooms in Outlook as usual.
